# Research digest: What is AI Engineering, what is this role and what a AI Engineer do

## TL;DR
- AI Engineering is an emerging technical discipline focused on designing, developing, and deploying scalable, efficient, and reliable AI-based applications [1].
- The role heavily centers on building products and applications using pre-trained foundation models (such as large language models) provided as a service [3].
- While Data Scientists analyze data for insights and Machine Learning Engineers build and train models in-house, AI Engineers primarily focus on putting AI into real-world applications [15].
- Core technical components span data infrastructure, algorithm selection, deep learning, NLP, and system security [1].

## Key findings

### Role Definition and Distinctions
- **AI Engineer vs. Data Scientist vs. ML Engineer:** A Data Scientist studies data to uncover insights using heavy statistics; a Machine Learning Engineer builds, trains, and deploys custom models from scratch requiring deep math and coding; an AI Engineer puts AI into real-world applications, often leveraging ready-made foundation models and APIs [15].
- **Focus on Foundation Models:** AI engineering is often characterized as software engineering specialized in Generative AI, where practitioners mainly work with foundational models developed by specialized research labs [3].
- **Practical Blending:** Most production systems combine both paradigms. For instance, a customer support chatbot might use a generative foundation model for responses alongside locally built components like request classifiers or scoring mechanisms [3]. Because of this integration, the terms AI engineering and machine learning engineering are frequently used interchangeably in practice [3].

### Core Responsibilities and Technical Scope
- **Development Lifecycle:** Responsibilities span the entire AI lifecycle, from data preparation and model integration to deployment, system monitoring, and collaboration with cross-functional teams [2].
- **Data Engineering & Infrastructure:** Involves gathering diverse datasets from databases and APIs, managing ETL pipelines, cleaning and normalizing data, and utilizing cloud services or distributed computing to maintain scalability and security [7, 8, 9, 10].
- **Optimization and Application:** Engineers optimize algorithms and adjust hyperparameters [13, 14], utilize transfer learning to adapt pre-trained models [15], and implement natural language processing (NLP) tasks using transformer architectures like BERT and GPT.

## Uncertainties and conflicting claims
- Sources note a distinct terminology overlap: while strict definitions separate AI engineering (building applications with third-party foundation models) from machine learning engineering (building and deploying in-house models) [3], industry practices often blur these lines because real-world systems regularly integrate both approaches [3].
